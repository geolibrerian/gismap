Recognize a service URL

ArcGIS REST URLs commonly end in FeatureServer, MapServer, or ImageServer. A number such as /0 identifies one layer inside a multi-layer service. GISMap can inspect a service root and, when needed, let you choose a sublayer.

Open a service

Choose Data, then Add ArcGIS REST service. Paste the HTTPS endpoint, review any discovered layers, and add the one you need. Public servers must permit cross-origin browser requests; private services may require an ArcGIS connection.

Choose the right endpoint

Use FeatureServer for queryable vector features and tables, MapServer for server-rendered cartography and map sublayers, and ImageServer for imagery or raster products. Check the publisher metadata for scale, fields, refresh schedule, and terms.

Privacy by design. GISMap runs in the browser. Remote services are requested from their publishers, and local files stay on the device unless you choose to export or share them elsewhere.
