Field task · assignment and report links

A field task and its report, sent as two links

A field task sends someone to places on the map with instructions and a checklist. The assignment travels as a link or QR code. In the field, they check in, tick off steps and places, write what they found, and send back a report link. No account, server or app install is needed on either side.

Open the taskMore examples

What it shows off

  • Features from a public layer (two ballparks, by object ID) to visit and mark done
  • Steps to tick off, and notes for the person doing the work
  • Check-in: where and when the visit happened, with GPS accuracy
  • The report link back: steps done, places visited, notes, who and when

Make it by hand

  1. Open Workflows, then Field tasks, and choose + New field task.
  2. Write the title, instructions and steps. Choose Attach a feature from the map for each place to visit (up to five).
  3. Choose Share to send the link or show its QR code.
  4. The person in the field opens it, checks in, ticks off steps and places, writes a report and sends the link back. Opening that link shows the report.

Or generate it

Field tasks can come out of the work you have already done:

  • From a briefing: an Actions slide's next steps become a field task with Send these as a field task.
  • And back again: a task's Briefing and Tour buttons turn its features into a briefing or a tour.

Check before you share

  • Everything is in the link, so anyone with it can read it: keep private details out of tasks and reports.
  • A task holds up to 5 features, 10 steps and 500 characters of notes, so its link fits a QR code.
  • The report here is a sample, written for this example; the app shows its times in your time zone.
