This is the stable dataset behind GISMap’s Historical Quakes example briefing. The guided version loads its source-authored magnitude symbols, an M6.5+ map scope, editable event slides, and an observed-time cinematic clip. It then walks through the source, style, filter, slides, playback, and sharing controls.

What this dataset contains

A stable historical earthquake archive published as an ArcGIS FeatureServer, with source-authored magnitude symbols. Explore seismic history, identify large events, and build a source-styled, observed-time briefing without relying on a changing feed.
