Publish what you configured

Put a live GISMap view on your own site.

A map, briefing, or guided tour can travel as one self-contained public link. Choose Embed on a website in GISMap and paste the generated iframe where your site accepts HTML.

Three viewer types

Use the view that matches the audience.

Configured web map

Interactive map

Publish the map’s public layers, cartography, filters, camera, basemap, and interface look. Visitors can pan and zoom without seeing project or authoring controls.

In GISMap: Share & export → Share map → Embed on a website.

Briefing

Presentation with place

Share the same public data, selected slides, filters, map context, and visual treatment. It opens directly into the presentation rather than an editing workspace.

In GISMap: Briefing → Share → Embed on a website.

Guided tour

Chapter-by-chapter story

Embed a focused, camera-only tour with its playback controls. It is ideal for a story page, exhibit, course, or campaign landing page.

In GISMap: Tours → Share & export → Embed this tour on a website.

What gets pasted

One responsive iframe.

GISMap writes a standard iframe. The URL remains the source of truth, so the embedded view is the same shared map, briefing, or tour—not a screenshot or a parallel copy.

<iframe src="https://gismap.online/?embed=1#map=…"
  title="Community map" loading="lazy"
  allow="fullscreen; geolocation" allowfullscreen
  style="width:100%;height:600px;border:0"></iframe>

Adjust only the height if your page needs a taller or shorter frame. Keep the generated URL intact so its configured state is preserved.

Before you publish

Public links need public sources.

Use web-accessible dataOnly public remote layers can be carried in a share link. Local files, sign-ins, credentials, and browser-only projects are intentionally not embedded.
Review the exact viewFilters, styles, camera, basemap, and briefing selection travel in the link. Open the generated embed URL in a private browser window before publishing.
Expect live data to stay liveEmbedded maps draw from their published sources. If the source changes, is restricted, or is removed, the embedded view changes or cannot load.
Use a normal link for sharing people-to-peopleQR codes and share links are better for messages and handoffs. The iframe is specifically for a page you control.
Need a portable record instead? Use the Share & export panel to download a map PDF, briefing handout, tour file, or project package. Those are better for archives and offline transfer than a live embed.
