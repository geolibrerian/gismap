Discover / AI / Publish with GISMap SI
Experimental · needs GISMap SI alpha access

From a stored dataset to a verified share link.

GISMap SI stores your organisation's data. AI Map in GISMap Online turns it into a Brief: slides on a live map, written with the AI model you connect. When the Brief is ready, you publish it, and GISMap SI checks that the link opens for the people you send it to.

During the GISMap SI alpha, signing in and public share links are switched on only for people on the alpha list. Everything else in GISMap Online works without an account, as before.

How it works

Five steps, and you press Publish.

Start in GISMap SI

On a dataset in Stored Intel, choose Create in GISMap Online. GISMap SI makes a draft that only your workspace can open and sends you to it in AI Map. A dataset that has no public link yet needs its owner or an admin to publish it first.

Sign in to GISMap SI

GISMap Online asks you to Sign in to GISMap SI in a small window. The connection lasts eight hours, stays in this browser tab only, and never goes into a link, a project file or a Smart Map Image. Sign out ends it.

Ask AI Map for a Brief

The assistant reads your datasets' titles, fields and dates from GISMap SI as reference data, checks each field before using it, and builds slides you can edit like any other. It never makes up figures.

Review in the Publish panel

Open Publish…. It shows who can open the link (anyone with it), whether figures are live or pinned, each layer and where it comes from, and the figures themselves. The assistant can open this panel for you, but only you can publish.

Publish and read the check

GISMap SI saves the version you reviewed, makes the share link, and checks it: that the link opens, that each layer answers, and that the slides' fields exist. Each check's result is shown as GISMap SI wrote it.

Live or pinned

Choose what viewers see when the data changes.

Live

Figures recompute

Numbers on the slides are worked out again each time the Brief plays. Later edits reach viewers when you Republish to this link; the link stays the same.

Pinned

Figures as of today

Numbers are frozen when you publish and shown “as of” that date, which suits a Brief for a meeting. If the data changes later, viewers are told so. To show newer figures, publish a new link.

Either way

Viewers see what you published

A share link always shows the version you published, never your draft in progress. The draft stays visible only to your workspace.

Links

Two addresses to know.

Your draft

gismap.online/m/<id>

Opens the draft for editing if you can edit it, or read-only otherwise. Add ?app=ai-map to open it in AI Map. You need to be signed in to GISMap SI.

The share link

gismap.online/m/<id>?p=<publication>

Opens the published version in the viewer, never the editor. Anyone with the link can open it without signing in, once GISMap SI's public links are on.

Not yet

What this doesn't do yet.

Only “anyone with the link”

Links for your team only, or for named people, come later.

Layers keep their data

Until GISMap SI can add new data to an existing dataset, a live Brief's layers show the data their links served when published; only its figures recompute.

Tours and Cinematic

Publishing covers maps and Briefs. Tours and Cinematic animations come later.
