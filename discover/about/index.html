About GISMap

Spatial tools should be easier to open, understand, and share.

GISMap Online is a free, open-source, browser-based GIS workspace for exploring data and creating practical spatial deliverables without starting with a desktop installation.

Open by default

Use public data and open formats whenever possible. Inspect a map before relying on it.

Keep people in control

Work in the browser and keep a clear distinction between local work, shared links, and publisher data.

Make results portable

Maps, briefings, tours, and field handoffs should be readable and usable by the people they are for.
