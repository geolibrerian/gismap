GIS MAP ONLINE
Free, open-source browser GIS

Free browser-based GIS maps

GISMap.online is a free, open-source, browser-based GIS workspace for people and AI agents. Open ArcGIS REST services, Geo URLs such as GeoJSON and GeoParquet, or local files; inspect, style and share interactive 2D and 3D maps without installing desktop GIS software. Remote services must allow browser access, and supported features depend on the source and map engine.
